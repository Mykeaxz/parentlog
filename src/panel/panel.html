    ParentLog
    
      
    Turn a parent email you already sent into a PowerSchool log entry — and keep your own list of every contact.
    
      
      
        
    1. Allow your PowerSchool site.
          
      
          
      
        
      2. 
        
    2. In Gmail or Outlook, open the email and click Log this contact.
      3. 
        
    3. In PowerSchool, open the student → Submit Log Entry. A ParentLog box appears — click Fill this form, check it, click Submit.
      4. 
      
    
      
    Nothing leaves your browser. Student and parent text is never sent anywhere.
