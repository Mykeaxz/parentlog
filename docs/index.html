Free during the pilot · Gmail and Outlook · Nothing leaves your browser

Step 1

Click "Log this contact"

In Gmail or Outlook, on the email you sent or received. ParentLog reads that one message, nothing else.

Step 2

Open the student in PowerSchool

Go to Submit Log Entry as usual. ParentLog fills Date, Log Type, Title and the entry text.

Step 3

Check it, click Submit

You always review first. ParentLog never submits for you, and marks the contact as saved once PowerSchool confirms.
