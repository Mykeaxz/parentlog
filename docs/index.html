ParentLog

Parent emails → PowerSchool log entries. One click. You review. You submit.

ParentLog is a Chrome extension for teachers. It copies a parent email you already sent into your school's PowerSchool log-entry form and keeps your own ledger of parent contacts. Nothing leaves your browser.
